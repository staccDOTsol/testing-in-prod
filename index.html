questions

Before you burn the sandbox down

Isn't testing in production risky?

Less risky than a staging environment that lies to you. Guardrails, traffic ramping, and one-click rollback mean real users see your change for seconds, not days, if something's wrong.

Do I need to change my whole pipeline?

No. Point your existing deploy step at testing-in-prod and it handles ramping, monitoring, and rollback for you.

What happens if a rollout fails?

Automatic guardrails catch error-rate and latency regressions and roll back before most users ever see them — no pager required.

Can I keep a staging environment too?

Sure, if you like paying for two environments that both eventually have to be tested against reality anyway.
